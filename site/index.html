How it works

Three steps and it's already working.

Install it on your computer. If you want, it keeps working in the cloud. And you control everything from your phone.

1

Install on your computer free

It uses your files and programs, with your own AI key or the subscription you already have.

2

Move it to the cloud, if you want Pro

With your computer off, it keeps handling tasks. You choose what runs where.

3

Control it from your phone

Scan a QR code once, then chat, approve and follow everything from wherever you are.

Private connection. Your phone, your computer and the cloud talk over an encrypted connection. Nobody in between can see the content, not even us.

Control

It's fast, but you are in charge.

An assistant that touches your emails and files needs clear limits. Calango has several.

Asks for your OK first

Sending, deleting, paying or publishing only after you say yes. Afterwards, it shows what was done.

Your data stays with you

Conversations, files and passwords stay on your computer or your cloud. They don't go through our servers.

Spending limit

You set how much each task can spend on AI. When it hits the limit, it stops and tells you.

Remote control only if you want

Viewing and controlling your screen from your phone is off by default. When you turn it on, a notice appears.

FAQ

Before you ask.

Do I have to pay to use it?

No. Everything that runs on your computer is free. Pro is only for people who want the assistant working in the cloud.

Which AI does it use?

The one you choose. You can use your own AI key or tools you already subscribe to, such as Claude Code and Codex.

Do you read my conversations?

No. Your conversations and files stay with you. Our servers only handle the account, the license and updates.

How do I reach my computer away from home?

From your phone, over an encrypted connection. You connect once by scanning a QR code, and that's it.

What if the computer shuts down in the middle of a task?

If the task is set to run in the cloud, it keeps going. If not, it runs again when the computer comes back, without repeating what it already did.

When does it launch?

It's under construction. The first version will be for Windows computers. Join the waitlist to find out when it's out.
